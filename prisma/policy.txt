==================================================
1. RETURN POLICY
==================================================

Customers may request a return within 15 days of receiving an eligible product.
The item must be unused, unwashed, and returned with original tags, packaging,
and accessories.

Items that are personalized, used, damaged after delivery, or missing original
tags may not qualify for return.

For hygiene reasons, certain innerwear, swimwear, and opened personal-care
products are non-returnable.

To request a return, customers should open the Orders section of their account,
select the relevant order, and choose "Request Return". Return approval is
subject to product eligibility and inspection.


==================================================
2. EXCHANGE POLICY
==================================================

Eligible clothing and footwear can be exchanged within 15 days for a different
size or, where available, a different color of the same product.

Exchange requests depend on inventory availability.

If the requested replacement size is unavailable, the customer may be offered
a refund or store credit according to the applicable order and payment method.


==================================================
3. REFUND POLICY
==================================================

Once a returned item passes inspection, refunds are normally initiated within
3 business days.

The actual time for the money to appear depends on the payment provider or bank.

For prepaid orders, refunds are generally sent back to the original payment
method.

For eligible cash-on-delivery orders, the customer may be asked to provide
valid bank or UPI details.


==================================================
4. SHIPPING & DELIVERY
==================================================

Standard delivery generally takes 3–7 business days after dispatch.

Remote locations may require additional time.

Customers can track an order from the Orders page using the tracking link shown
after the shipment is dispatched.

If an order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window, customers should
contact support with their order number.


==================================================
5. CANCELLATION POLICY
==================================================

An order can normally be cancelled before it enters the shipping process.

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.
The customer may instead use the applicable return process after delivery.


==================================================
6. PRODUCT & SIZE INFORMATION
==================================================

Product pages contain age recommendations, size charts, materials, care
instructions, and other available details.

Parents and guardians should review age and size information before purchasing.

If a child is between sizes, customers should use the measurements in the
product-specific size chart rather than relying only on age-based sizing.

7. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
==================================================

Q: What is LittleNest Kids?
A: LittleNest Kids is a fictional children's e-commerce store used in this
demo. It sells kids' clothing, footwear, toys, bags, and accessories.


Q: How long do I have to return an item?
A: Eligible products can be requested for return within 15 days of delivery.


Q: Can I exchange a product for another size?
A: Yes. Eligible clothing and footwear can be exchanged for another available
size within the exchange window.


Q: Can I return an opened toy?
A: A toy that has been used or damaged may not qualify for return. Eligibility
depends on its condition and the product's return rules.


Q: How can I check my order status?
A: Log in to your account, open the Orders section, and select the relevant
order to view its current status and tracking information.


Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery generally takes 3–7 business days after dispatch.


Q: When will I get my refund?
A: After a returned product passes inspection, the refund is normally initiated
within 3 business days. Bank or payment-provider processing can take additional
time.


Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can normally be cancelled before they enter the shipping process.
Cancellation may not be possible after dispatch.


Q: What if the product I received is damaged?
A: Contact customer support as soon as possible and provide the order number
and photos of the damaged product and packaging.


Q: What if I ordered the wrong size?
A: If the item is eligible and within the exchange window, you can request an
exchange for an available size.


Q: Are all products returnable?
A: No. Some personalized products and certain hygiene-sensitive products may
be non-returnable.


Q: Does the chatbot know my order status?
A: The chatbot can provide order-specific information only when it is connected
to the store's authenticated order system and the customer has permission to
access that order.


Q: Can the chatbot recommend products?
A: Yes. A chatbot can recommend products using product attributes, catalog
data, and semantic search over product descriptions.


Q: Can I ask about washing instructions?
A: Yes. If care instructions are stored in the product information or
knowledge base, the chatbot can retrieve and explain them.


Q: How do I contact customer support?
A: For this demo, customers can contact support through the Help & Support
section of the website.
