RETURNS AND REFUNDS

How returns work on Bachpankart: many products on Bachpankart can be returned within the window shown on the product page (typically up to 7 days from delivery for eligible items). Return eligibility depends on the product category and seller policy shown at checkout.

Before you return an item:
- Keep items unused, with tags and original packaging where possible.
- Note the return window on your order confirmation and product page.
- Contact us if an item arrived damaged or incorrect — we'll prioritise your case.

Refund timing: approved refunds are processed to your original payment method. Prepaid orders are refunded after we receive and inspect the return. COD (cash on delivery) refunds may be issued via bank transfer or store credit as per our support team's guidance.

SHIPPING AND DELIVERY

Delivery areas: Bachpankart delivers across India to PIN codes we service at checkout. Enter your pincode on the site to see delivery options for your area.

Delivery charges:
- A flat shipping charge may apply on smaller orders.
- Orders above the free-shipping threshold (shown at checkout) qualify for FREE delivery.
- Multi-seller orders may ship in separate packages; each shipment is priced transparently in your order summary.

Delivery timelines: most orders reach metro cities within 3–7 business days after dispatch. Remote areas can take longer. You'll get SMS updates when your order ships.

ORDER TRACKING

Order updates: once you place an order, we send updates by SMS as your package moves through packing, dispatch, and delivery. You can also check the status of your order anytime in your account.

How to track an order:
- Sign in with the phone number you used at checkout.
- Go to My account → Orders.
- Open the order to see its current status and shipment details.

Order statuses explained:
- Placed — we've received your order.
- Processing — the seller is preparing your items.
- Shipped — on the way, with tracking when available.
- Delivered — handed over at your address.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Ordering and payment:
- Do I need an account? Yes. Sign in with your mobile number and OTP to checkout and track orders.
- What payment methods do you accept? Cash on delivery (COD, where available) and online payment via our secure payment partner.
- Can I change my address after ordering? Contact support as soon as possible, before the order ships.

Products and sellers:
- Are products authentic? We work with verified sellers and curated catalog data.
- Why did my order come in multiple packages? Items may ship from different sellers; each shipment is tracked separately.
- How do sizes work? Use the size guide on the product page when available.

Selling on Bachpankart:
- How do I become a seller on Bachpankart? Go to https://seller.bachpankart.com, click Start selling, and complete registration with your business and KYC details.
- Where do sellers log in? At https://seller.bachpankart.com/login.
- More details: see the Sell on Bachpankart page at /sell, which links to the seller portal.

Account and wishlist:
- How do I save items? Tap the heart on a product while signed in to add it to your wishlist.
- How do I update my profile? Go to My account → Profile to edit your name and email.
